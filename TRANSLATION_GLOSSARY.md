## Style Notes

- Keep short button labels direct and action-oriented.
- Keep KPI labels compact because cards and table headers have limited space.
- Keep common trading abbreviations such as P&L and R when they improve scan speed.
- Pair English originals with localized terms only for concepts that traders often recognize by the English term.
- Error messages should explain what the user can do next.
- Empty states should point to the next useful action, such as adding or importing trades.
